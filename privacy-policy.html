AutoSetu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

AutoSetu is an automation service that helps Instagram business and creator accounts respond to enquiries. When an account owner connects their Instagram account to AutoSetu and authorizes it through Instagram's official login, we receive and process the following data through Meta's official APIs:

  • The connected account's Instagram user ID and username.
  • Direct messages sent to the connected account, and comments on its posts and reels — only to match them against reply rules the account owner has configured.
  • An access token for the connected account, stored securely and used only to send the automated replies the owner configured.

We use this data solely to operate the automation the account owner set up. We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except Meta's platforms as needed to send replies. Message and comment content is processed to find rule matches and is retained only as operational logs, which the account owner may ask us to delete at any time.

Data Deletion

To delete your data: disconnect the AutoSetu app from your Instagram account (Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → AutoSetu → Remove), and email us or message the AutoSetu Instagram account (@auto_setu) asking for deletion. We will delete your stored token, rules and logs within 30 days, and confirm when done. Removing the app also immediately stops all automation for your account.

Questions about this policy: message us on Instagram at @auto_setu.
